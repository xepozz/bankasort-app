Support

Do you have a problem, or did you find a bug? Write to xepozz@icloud.com. Tell us your device model, the iOS version and the level number.

How to play

  1. Tap a jar to take its top layer of goods.
  2. Tap a different jar to pour the goods into it. You can pour into an empty jar or onto the same kind of goods.
  3. A jar with one kind of goods closes with a lid. Close all the jars to clear the level.

Use fewer pours to get more stars. When you are stuck, use the hint, undo, "Add jar" or "Free pour".

Questions

Why can I not pour into this jar?

The top goods in that jar are a different kind, or the jar has no free space. An empty jar takes all kinds of goods. A locked jar stays locked until the first jar on the board closes with a lid.

Can I get stuck with no way to win?

Each level has a solution. We checked all 200 levels. When no move is left, undo a move, add a jar or restart the level. The hint looks for a move from the current board.

Where is my progress saved?

The game saves it automatically on your device. You do not need an account. The progress does not move between an iPhone and an iPad.

I installed the game again, and my progress is gone

The progress stays only inside the app, so it goes when you remove the app. To get it back, restore the full device from a backup that you made before the removal. You can open any level again: tap the level plate at the top of the screen. Your Game Center achievements and scores stay in your Game Center profile.

How do I open Game Center?

Tap the gear button at the top left to open Settings. Then tap "Game Center" to see the leaderboards and achievements. This button shows when you are signed in to Game Center in the Settings of your iPhone or iPad.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Only Game Center uses the internet to send your scores.

How do I turn off the sound?

Settings → Sound.

Which devices can run the game?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later. The game uses the landscape orientation.
