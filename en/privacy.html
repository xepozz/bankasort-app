Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version

Banka Sort collects no data. The game has no server of its own. It sends no data to the developer or to third parties.

What stays on your device

Only your progress: the current level, your stars, the game in progress and the sound setting. This data stays inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not send it anywhere.

What the app does not collect

The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reports, no ads and no tracking. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It has no in-app purchases.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Game Center, a service of Apple. When you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to it. Apple processes this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive this data. The game works fully without Game Center.

Backups

When you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, the backup includes the game progress, as for all apps. Apple operates these backups.

Removing your data

Remove the app, and the progress goes with it. You do not have to send a request: the developer has no copy.

Children

The game is for all ages. It collects no data about anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
