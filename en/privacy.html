Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

We collect data to improve the user experience. Three days after the install, the game shows an ad banner.

What is stored on your device

Your progress: the current level, your stars, the game in progress and the sound setting. This data is stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad.

Usage statistics

We collect data to improve the user experience.

Ads

The app shows no ads for the first 3 days after the install. After that, a banner of the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK) or of Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows at the bottom of the screen. Yandex picks the banner through its mediation. To show and count the ads, these SDKs get data about the device and the app, the IP address, impressions and taps. The app does not ask for tracking permission, and it tells the Yandex SDK to show no personalised ads. How Yandex uses the data: yandex.com/legal/confidential. How Google uses the data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What the app does not collect

The app has no account. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Apple handles the in-app purchases: the app does not get your payment details.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Game Center, a service of Apple. When you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to it. Apple processes this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. The game works fully without Game Center.

Backups

When you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, the backup includes the game progress, as for all apps. Apple operates these backups.

Removing your data

Remove the app, and the progress goes with it.

Children

The game is for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
