A puzzle for iPhone and iPad

Put everything in its jar

Cucumbers, apricots, tomatoes, sunflower seeds and nails are mixed up in big glass jars. Move them until each jar holds one kind of goods. Then the jar closes with a lid.

Coming soon to the App Store

How to play

  1. Tap a jar to take its top layer.
  2. Tap a different jar to pour the goods into it. You can pour into an empty jar or onto the same kind of goods.
  3. A jar with one kind of goods closes with a lid. Close all the jars to clear the level.

What is inside

200 levels

We checked each level. Each level has a solution.

Difficulty grows slowly

The first levels are a warm-up with few jars. Then come tricky, hard and ultra hard levels with a pour limit.

New twists

Jars that are not full, layers that hide under the top layer, and a locked jar. The lock opens when the first jar closes.

Stars

Use fewer pours to get more stars for the level.

Hint and undo

A hint shows a good move. Undo takes back the last move.

A spare jar

"Add jar" gives you one more empty jar. "Free pour" puts goods on top of a different kind. You can restart a level at any time.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements.

Nothing extra

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. The game works offline and collects no data.
